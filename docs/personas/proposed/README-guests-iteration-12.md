# The guests of crew iteration 12 — the lines their files would carry

Four builders worked this run and **none of them has a persona file**: el ebanista (the cabinetmaker),
la panadera (the baker), el inspector de línea (Rigo's trade, with Bash) and la mueblería (the furniture
shop). Each proposed, in its post-flight, the line a standing file should carry. There is nothing to
apply them to, so they are kept here, and in the ledger at the top of `docs/crew/FLIGHT-NOTES.md`,
against the day one of those characters is opened.

**El ebanista.** *"A chair is placed by its BACK: it faces the table or desk beside it, otherwise it
turns its back to the first wall and faces away — one rule seats a waiting corner and a doorstep alike.
And build to the person, then check the ROW: anything under 1.1 tall laid in the row before a south wall
is hidden from the default camera stop, so a chair built honestly can vanish where a box half-showed;
look at the frame before you promise it will be seen."*

**La panadera.** *"Before you build a thing, ask which SIDE of it names it at thirty-five pixels and lay
it that way — an oreja on its edge is a bun from above. And when a brief says a drawing 'tells the worlds
apart', grep the drawing for `world` before you believe it: the shelf read one seed and nothing else."*

**La mueblería.** *"A box is the one shape that can hide being a picture, so check its LID before you
believe it. Everything else in this engine confesses: a flat thing stands up like a sign and the audit
names it. A box does not — it has sides, it takes light, it passes every check a real box passes, and the
drawing it wears on top is the plan of itself lying face-up. Crop the top of it at 3× before you agree
that it is finished. And the other half of the same habit: when you make one thing in a room better,
re-read what the room's guard was counting, because a check written when crates were the cure counts
crates, and the day the last crate becomes furniture it will go red on a room where nothing is wrong."*

**El inspector de línea** proposed an edit to `.claude/agents/rigo.md`, which is a file that exists;
it went to `proposed/rigo/2026-09-21-claude-e9a5.md` and the session refused it, with the reason there.
