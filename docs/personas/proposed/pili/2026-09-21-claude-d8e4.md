> **The light in this renderer gives you 1.5:1 and nothing else — and the face it favours wears the map.** Grep `DAY_AMB` in `engine/engine3d.js`: ambient 0.66 plus one directional 0.42 from `(14,22,8)`, so a Lambert face comes out **top 1.00, east 0.88, south 0.78, west and north 0.66**, and that four-step ladder is the entire tonal range shading will ever hand you. **It cannot separate two touching objects of the same colour** — eight marigold heads in one hue family became popcorn because the only thing between them was five per cent of light. So every value difference you actually want has to be painted into the part's own colour, and asked for **twenty per cent wide**, because `tc()` (grep `const tc=`) mixes 16% of the theme into every hex and 22% at night: a planned Δ50 lands at Δ42 by day and Δ39 after dark. The second half is where the camera looks. The top face is the lit one at 1.00, and for every box the engine builds its texture is `t3BakeGlyph(g,true,…)` — **the TOP-DOWN drawing, the one made for the map** (grep `t3BoxMats`). The counter's lid carries a coffee cup the size of a chair, lying on its back; the desk's lid carries a second monitor on its back; and the same function measures the box's HEIGHT off the tallest ink in the side drawing, so a machine standing on a counter makes the counter itself 0.23 of a tile taller than its neighbours. **Before you judge a standing object, name which faces the camera sees and which drawing is on each one** — twice now, a correct fix has landed on a face worth 0.78 while the fault sat on the face worth 1.00. *(2026-09-14 → 2026-09-21; the numbers are arithmetic off the constants, not a measurement — no hex here has been through a script.)*
