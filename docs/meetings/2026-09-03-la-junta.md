## 1. Telling people apart

**The call: turn the job icons back on. Two lines of code, minutes.**

Here's what we found. Every neighbour already has a little floating icon for their trade — Chelo's basket, Tacho's wrench, Chava's knife. It's in the game today. But the code says: *if this person has something to say, draw the red mark; otherwise draw their icon.* Never both. So the 28 people who actually give you work are the only ones in town whose job you never see. And even for the three who don't give quests, the icon is only on screen 2.4 seconds out of every 13.

Draw the icon **beside** the red mark instead of instead of it, and widen that window. That's it. Thirty-one people get their job back, in all four cameras, at emoji resolution — which beats anything we could hand-draw at six pixels. Same commit: the theme currently mixes 40% of its colour into every shirt in town, which actively erases the one difference the barrio has. Drop it to 15%.

**Then look at it before we spend anything else.** Nacho wanted 28 hand-drawn props; Pili wanted a hat-and-apron vocabulary with a silhouette test; Don Güero wanted body builds. All three are good and all three are a full sitting or more. The icon fix is the control experiment: if the barrio reads after it, we've saved a week.

**Refused:** floating name plates. The code exists but only in two of the four cameras, and in the camera the game actually boots into a 12-character name is 58 pixels wide on a 36-pixel sprite. It would clip. Names already arrive when someone speaks.

**Seam:** `engine.js:889`, `:801`, `:402`, `engine3d.js:285` — the same four-line branch, four times.

## 2. What is this business, and what is "the assistant"

**The call: build readable objects — and make the first one the laptop on the old lead's desk. A sitting.**

You're right, and the reason is structural: **Meridian can only be read by talking to somebody.** The engine has a function that finds people, and a function that finds animals. Nothing finds a thing. Every explanation the city owns is locked inside a person, so if you haven't found the right neighbour yet, you're standing in a room that won't tell you what it is.

So: an object you can stand next to and read, with its own marker — a cream card shape that *breathes* rather than bounces, so it never gets confused with the red mark over a head. It rides the exact code path the door arrow already uses, which means it exists in all four cameras on day one, free.

**One correction to the table, and it matters.** Three experts proposed building these as "decor." Decor is drawn in only two of the four cameras, and the game boots into one of the other two. We'd have shipped six notes you can't see. Build them as **wall tiles** instead — the office window pane already proves a drawing can live inside a wall and show up correctly in 3D.

**First object, not sixth: the laptop.** The missing lead's machine, still logged in, three short paragraphs in handwriting that isn't yours — what the assistant is, what the sheet is, what the book is. Those words appear 91 times across the quests and are defined nowhere you can walk to.

One card per business after that is real writing — six businesses, four lines each, English and Spanish. That's a Nacho commission, not part of this sitting.

**Seam:** a sibling to `checkTalk()` at `engine.js:1735`; a Read button (the button rail already has five tiers, there's a free slot); `readMarks()` beside `doorMarks()` at `:1726`.

## 6. Realism, and a skill for Don Güero

**The call: the thing to build isn't a skill, it's the seam a skill would police — the world may read a grade. A sitting, and it unblocks items 4 and 5.**

"Realistic" splits two ways and the table was unanimous on the order.

**The skill itself** goes inside the Don Güero skill that already exists — not a second one beside it — and its first rule is the refusal: *name what the player would see change, and name what in the save it reads from; refuse any change the content pack cannot declare.*

**Ship this week, separately, in minutes:** the screenshot test already takes a camera per shot. Make it loop all four. That single check would have caught the invisible mural, and it retires that whole class of bug permanently.

## WHAT THIS PROJECT NOW KNOWS

**EVERY PLACE OWES ONE THING YOU CAN READ WITHOUT TALKING.** Written in the place's own voice, never addressed to the player.
