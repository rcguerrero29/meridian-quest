## 1 · What the map is today `[CODE]`

`drawTown` (`engine/engine.js`, grep `function drawTown`) paints the tiles of **one** world
(`PL.street`) from the pack's colours, then the pack's labels (`TOWNLBL`), **each gated on
`when(flags)`** over `worldFlags()` — which already carries `answered`, `grade`, `closed`, `up` per
district — then a fixed legend line at 7.5 CSS px, then **one** dot from `MAPDOT` for where you stand.
`openMap` fills `#mapov` and adds the name of the room you are in. **Nothing on it reads quest state.**
The state it needs exists: `done`, `qOpen(i)`, `pendingAt(n)`, `worldPending(id)`, `hasSay(n)`.
Measured: 9.39 CSS px per tile at 390 wide; two `MAPDOT` anchors are shared by several worlds; `pa`,
`li`, `ex` have no anchor; **Calle Dos is not on the plan at all** (Rosa §2.2) — a whole street the
plan cannot show, whatever marks it grows.

## 2 · The recommendation — presence on the plan, never a list

All five advisers converge on one shape and refuse the other two:

- **A · Marks on the plan.** *Chosen.* On every door or anchor whose world has somebody waiting
  (`worldPending`), and on every person standing on a drawn world who has something to say
  (`hasSay`), draw **the same ❗ the street draws** — one mark, one meaning, the mark the player
  already knows. Nothing else: no count, no colour ramp, no age, no label.
- **B · A list under the plan.** *Refused, by construction.* It is the banned object of
  `docs/ARCH-LOG.md` A3 — a to-do list with the town as decoration; by day three the plan is
  decoration and the list is the game (Tavo, Rosa, the sweep's M2: *a count is a list with the names
  removed*).
- **C · Both.** Refused: teaches B, because when a picture and a list say the same thing people read
  the list.

**Two marks the crew cut before anyone builds them:** a "finished" tick (the lot→building change
already carries the past, and a third mark on a 9-px plan is the noise the 2026-09-03 junta refused)
and any mark that changes with age or chapter (`docs/OWNER.md`, the mark means one thing forever).

**The one call left open for the owner — a ring on "next".** The mock rendered in the real game
(journey page, screen 12; `docs/mocks/2026-09-14-la-sobremesa/journey/j-mapafter-…`) rings the next
quest. Tavo would keep a single "next"; Rosa cuts it (*no highlight, not a pulse, not a bigger pin*);
the sweep's M3 says Meridian already orders "next" at district level — one district open at a time —
so presence gives it for free and a ring only earns its place inside a district, where `qOpen` has no
order. **Recommendation: build presence first; decide the ring after he has played the plan with
marks on it for a week.** Both sides are cited in the meeting file (Tavo §PART 2; Rosa §2.5 step 3;
the sweep, M3).

## 7 · The owner's word — 2026-09-15 — a hybrid, colour by kind, and the legend comes back

*Verbatim in `docs/ASKS.md`, 2026-09-15 (second window): **"oh ok you are right on halo, but ok i like
a hybrid approach, wehre i can choose my destination, and i like the marking of people with quests in
different colors but its hard to tell  unless you have a legend."** It answers the Halo question of the
same morning (`docs/research/2026-09-14-map-and-questionnaires.md`) and it overrules this plan twice.
Written down as an overruling, not averaged.*

### 7.1 · What he overruled, and what he did not

| | |
|---|---|
| **Overruled: no colour** | §2's chosen shape A (*one mark, one meaning… no colour ramp*) and the sweep's **M2** (*never a count, colour or age*). He has seen coloured marks, he likes them, and the fault he reports is not the colour — it is that **nothing tells him what a colour means** |
| **Overruled: cut the legend** | §4 and the sweep's **M5** (*the mark is its own legend, a tap names the place*). A legend was cut on the reasoning that one mark needs none. With more than one mark that reasoning expires, and his sentence is the measurement |
| **NOT overruled: WCAG 1.4.1** | *"Color is not used as the only visual means of conveying information"*, with about 1 in 12 men affected (both `(read)`, the research file). This is not a crew opinion he can overrule by preference, and it does not need to be: **it is the same complaint he made.** So every colour also differs in **shape**, and the legend it needs is the one 1.4.1 already required |
| **NOT overruled: the mark means one thing forever** | `docs/OWNER.md`, `docs/STORY.md:57`. This is the line that decides what colour is *allowed to say* — see 7.2 |
| **NOT overruled: A3, never a list** | `docs/ARCH-LOG.md` A3. Colour that says *what kind of person* is not a list. Colour that says *how many, how old, how far through* is a list with the names removed, which is what M2 was actually protecting |

### 7.2 · Colour says KIND, never STATE — and the engine already computes the kinds

`hasSay` (`engine/engine.js`, grep `const hasSay`) is three clauses ORed together, and each is a
different reason a person is standing there:

| Clause | The kind | What the mark is |
|---|---|---|
| `pendingAt(n)` | somebody has **work** for you | the ❗ the street already draws — unchanged, and it stays the one the player knows |
| `roomPending(n)` | the **host of this room** has a question | ❗ today; a mark of its own kind here |
| `n.doc` | there is **something to read** | the breathing cream card `drawReadMark` already exists (`engine/engine.js`, grep `function drawReadMark`) — *"a cream card that BREATHES — never the bouncing ❗"*. **The kind split he is asking for is half-built and eleven days old** |

That is three kinds, each with a shape this game already draws, and it answers #160's own open question
(*does an ungraded conversation get the same mark as a quest?* — #157) with a place to put the answer
rather than a fourth colour invented on the day. **State stays out:** no age, no count, no progress, no
"finished" tick (§2 cut that one before anyone built it, and that cut holds).

**The trap, named:** `worldFlags` hands the plan `grade` (`engine/engine.js:4741-4750`) and its own
comment warns *not begun is not done well*. Colour-by-grade will arrive as a one-line improvement from
somebody who reads this section as permission. It is not — it is state, and R16 is about exactly that.

### 7.3 · "I can choose my destination" — the hybrid, and why Halo was the wrong reference

Halo's tracker is **egocentric and about danger** — it answers *what is near me right now*, on a screen
you never open, and it has no idea what you are trying to do. That is why it is dynamic and that is why
it cannot say where a quest is. What he actually described is the other half of the family the sweep
catalogued: **you pick a destination, and then the world points, rather than the map leading.**

- **Precedents, both already cited:** Elden Ring's Guidance of Grace is *a direction, not a
  destination*; AC Odyssey's Exploration mode gives *region plus bearing*; Ghost of Tsushima's wind
  gets you *"lost in nature, not in menus"* (the research file, the design-intent line).
- **This repo has already shipped the strong form of it, once.** El paseo: picking a destination in
  the Barrio Pass **rides you there** — bell, the line at `RIDE_ZIP`, the world changes at the end of
  the line. And the street already knows how to point: the door arrow within three steps
  (`engine/engine.js`, grep `drawDoorMark`) and the doorstep nudge (grep `The doorstep nudge`).
- **So the build is: tap a mark on the plan → that becomes my destination → the street carries a
  bearing to it until I get there, then it clears.** One destination at a time. Never a saved list of
  them, which is A3 again wearing a compass.

**It settles the call §2 left open.** The ring on "next" was a disagreement about whether the *game*
should nominate one — Tavo would, Rosa would not. His hybrid dissolves it: **the ring belongs to the
destination he chose.** Nothing nominates anything; nothing is highlighted until he picks it.

### 7.4 · The legend, and the fault under his sentence `[CODE]`

The plan draws *"a fixed legend line at 7.5 CSS px"* (§1, measured). **7.5 px is not a legend, and it
is very likely the thing he was squinting at when he wrote "its hard to tell".** The floor this project
already wrote for in-scene text applies (`engine/engine.js`, the block at `THE FLOOR FOR IN-SCENE
TEXT`): text that carries information may never exist only at a size nobody can read. So: the legend
returns as a **key with a swatch, a shape and a word per kind**, at panel type size, not plan type
size — three rows, two languages, the pack's own words.

### 7.5 · What this changes in the seam (§3 stands, with one row added)

| | What | Whose |
|---|---|---|
| RULE | `drawTown` paints presence marks **by kind** — the kind comes from which `hasSay` clause fired, the shapes are the ones the game already draws | engine |
| RULE | a destination a person picks, one at a time, and the street's bearing to it | engine |
| CHOICE | `TOWNPLAN` — unchanged from §3 | pack |
| CHOICE | which clauses count — unchanged from §3, and now it also chooses **which kinds get a colour at all** (the town declaring none stays byte-identical) | pack |
| content | the legend's three words per language; the anchors for `pa`, `li`, `ex` | pack |

**The guard gets one more red, and it is the one this project keeps failing:** not *"three colours were
used"* — a call count where the noun is a mark on a tile is `docs/REGRESSION.md` §3's commonest shape.
Read the pixels at a world's anchor with the clocks frozen, once per kind, and **plant a person who has
work AND a document** — two clauses true at once, which is a real state in Meridian — and insist the
plan draws the kind the rule says wins, not both on one tile.

### 7.6 · What is still his

1. **Three kinds, or two?** Work / room host / something to read — or fold the host into work, since a
   host with a question *is* work, and ship two.
2. **Where the legend lives:** a key drawn on the plan always, or behind the plan's `i`, opened once
   and remembered.
3. **Does the bearing show in the street, or only on the plan?** Rosa's attention argument (M8) is
   about corner minimaps, not about one arrow on a chosen destination — but it is his call, and it is
   the difference between *the world guides* and *the screen guides*.

## 8 · 2026-09-17 — he asked for the lit path §7.3 refused, and here is what changed

> *"lastly when you select some destination or person to chat with, can we do like a small light path
> for it"* — the owner, `docs/ASKS.md`, 2026-09-17

**§7.3 signs the opposite** — *"a DIRECTION, never a lit path"* — and the engine repeats it in a
comment above `BEARS`. **Reported, not absorbed**, which is this project's rule: the owner's word in
session wins, and the decision it overrides gets a dated line instead of a quiet edit. This is it.

**The case §7.3 made was real and is now cited** `[WEB]`
(`docs/research/2026-09-17-the-map-again.md` R4): Fable II's always-on golden trail drew exactly the
critique it was avoiding — a player's *"never got a sense of location … the golden path just leads
you to whatever you need to do next"*. Dead Space's line was the one players preferred, for one named
reason: **it was there only when you asked for it.**

**Both halves of that are already inside his sentence.** *"when you select"* — you asked for it.
*"a small light path"* — he said small. He described Dead Space's line, in his own words, without
having read either.

**What §7.3 keeps, in full.** The recommendation on the table
(`docs/plans/2026-09-17-the-map.md` §5) is that the route is drawn **on the paper, not in the
street**. A line on a map is what maps are for; a line painted on the ground is what Fable did.
§7.3's argument was about the *world* teaching you where you are, and the world is untouched: **the
street still carries a bearing and only a bearing.**

**And it pays for something §7.3 never had.** `planPlace` already walks the doors recursively to
answer *where is this world*; run the same walk from where you stand to what you picked and the
segments are the route. That makes the portal graph **visible** — a door that claims to lead
somewhere it does not now draws a route that visibly goes the wrong way — which is what
`docs/plans/2026-09-17-stairs-and-drawing.md` asked for and could only assert with a guard.

**Still his**, and flagged as his: whether the line may ever appear in the street. Nothing above
proposes that, and nothing above forecloses it.
