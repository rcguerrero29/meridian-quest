# For AJ — if you're drawing it 🎨

Hi AJ. You said the pictures were cute. They were, and that was the problem — so we'd rather you drew
them. This is everything you need to know so what you draw works in the game without anyone
redrawing it. **None of it is about style.** Your style is the point; these are the plumbing rules.

---

## 1 · The single most important thing: draw in LAYERS, not one picture

**Do not draw one flat picture of a finished dish.** Draw the parts separately, each on its own
transparent background, all lined up in the same frame:

    pot-body.png      the vessel, empty
    pot-contents.png  what's in it
    pot-steam.png     the steam, on its own
    pot-lid.png       the lid, if it has one
    pot-garnish.png   the scallion, the sesame, the thing on top

**Why:** the game can move layers. Steam that drifts up and fades, a lid that lifts, a garnish that
drops onto the dish when you sprinkle it, a gentle bob when the pot is set down — all of that is the
code moving your drawings. **It costs you nothing extra and it's most of why the games you play feel
alive.** One flat picture can only ever sit there.

If you only have the patience for two layers: **the dish, and the steam.** That alone buys the motion.

## 2 · Size, and the one number that matters

Draw at **384 × 384**, and check it looks right shrunk to **96 × 96**, because that's roughly how big
it is on a phone. If you can't tell what it is at 96, it doesn't matter how good it looks big.

- PNG, transparent background, no drop shadow baked in (the game draws the shadow, so it matches the room).
- Keep each layer the **same canvas size** and in the **same position**, so they stack without anyone nudging them.

## 3 · The light comes from the upper left. Always.

Every object in the game is lit from the **upper left**, about 40° off vertical. So:

- Highlights on the **upper-left** of a form. Shadow lower-right.
- **Light is warm, shadow is cool.** A shadow that's just a darker version of the same colour is what
  makes things look like plastic — let the shadow go slightly blue/purple, and the lit side slightly warm.
- **Don't draw a cast shadow underneath.** The game puts that in, so it sits properly on the counter.

If every dish obeys this, twelve dishes look like one kitchen. If they don't, they look like a
clip-art folder — and that's the difference a person notices without being able to say why.

## 4 · Colours we already know work

You can ignore this if you'd rather just draw — but these were measured against the paper colour so
things don't disappear:

| | |
|---|---|
| **White things** (rice, porcelain) | need a **grey-steel rim or edge**, or they vanish into the cream page — they're within 1 shade of it |
| **Dark things** (a black stew pot) | need the **steel rim** too, or they're a hole in the page |
| **Red chilli** | looks great on metal or on the page, **never directly on brown clay** — the two are too close |
| **The counter** | warm brown; your dish should be lighter or darker than it, not the same |

## 5 · What NOT to draw

- **No text in the picture.** Words in a drawing can't be translated, can't be copied, and vanish when
  she prints the recipe. Tell us the words and we'll set them in type.
- **No numbers, no stars, no hearts, no timers, no progress rings.** Nothing that scores anything.
- **No customers.** Nobody waiting for the food.
- **Nothing that looks sad if it's been left alone.** No wilting, no dust, no "you haven't cooked in a
  while" version. You said nothing should happen when you don't cook, and that has to be true in the art too.

## 6 · What to draw first — just three

Don't start with twelve. Start with **one dish, in layers**, and we'll put it in the game and show you
it moving. Then you'll know what you're drawing for.

After that, the three that teach us the most: **a stew in a pot** (deep vessel), **something cut and
laid out** (flat, many pieces), **something small and green in a shallow dish** — because between them
they cover almost every shape a Korean dish comes in.

## 7 · Where to put them

Drop them in a Google Drive folder and say the word. They get pulled straight into the game from there.
