# Questions for AJ

*Answer what you have opinions about. Skip the rest — blanks are fine and we'll ask
again once you've seen something on screen. Every one of these is changeable later.*

Each question is tagged with what it decides, so you can see what's riding on it.
The tags map to files in [AFFECTED.md](AFFECTED.md).

---

## The place

**1. Where is your game?** `[world]`
A town, a school, an island, a ranch, a neighborhood, a ship, a mall after hours —
anything. Meridian is a barrio with a main street and a canal. What's yours?

**2. What's it like to stand there?** `[world] [art]`
Time of day, weather, season, sound. Is it always golden hour? Always raining a
little? Snowing? Is there music playing somewhere?

**3. What's the first thing you'd do when you open it?** `[world] [loop]`
Not what you *should* do — what you'd actually want to do. Feed something? Water
something? Go see a specific person? Just walk?

## The people

**4. Name three people who live there.** `[cast]`
Who they are and one thing that's true about them. They can be based on real people.
Meridian has a foreman who quotes permits and abuelas with equal authority, and a
grandmother who runs a restaurant and knows the only recipe.

**5. Is there an animal?** `[cast]`
Meridian has a dog you can dress up, three cats, a parrot, a pigeon and a hummingbird.
You get at least one animal. What is it and what's its name?

**6. Who are you, in the game?** `[cast] [loop]`
New in town? Grew up there? Visiting? Do people know you already?

## What you do there

**7. What do people ask you for help with?** `[quests]`
In Meridian people bring you problems and you decide what to do. Yours could be
anything — recipes, mysteries, outfits, plants, lost things, feelings, homework,
gossip. What kind of problem shows up at your door?

**8. Is your game teaching anything, or is it just nice?** `[quests] [career]`
Both are completely valid answers. Meridian secretly teaches AI decision-making
because that's someone's job hunt. Yours does not have to teach anything at all.

**9. Do you want a mystery running underneath?** `[story]`
Something the town isn't telling you, that you unpick slowly. You like PLL and
Vampire Diaries, so this seems like a maybe — but mystery adds *tension*, and you
said cozy. Do those fit together for you, or does a mystery ruin the calm?

## The feel

**10. What makes a game feel cozy to you — specifically?** `[art] [loop]`
Name actual moments from games or shows. "The bit in ___ where ___." Specific beats
a much better guide than the word "cozy."

**11. What makes you quit a game?** `[loop] [grade]`
Genuinely useful. Timers? Losing progress? Being told you failed? Too much reading?
Not knowing what to do? Say the thing that annoys you.

**12. Should anything ever go wrong?** `[grade]`
Meridian is going the "nothing bad ever happens" route — you can get an answer wrong
and nothing is taken from you. Do you want the same, or do you want *some* stakes?

**14. What's missing from that list?** `[eggs]`
Books, music, games, YouTube, anything. It doesn't have to be a TV show.

## Practical

**15. What's it called?** `[world] [meta]`
The game, and the place. Placeholder names are fine.

**16. English only, or Spanish too?** `[i18n]`
Meridian is both, fully. Both is more writing but it's genuinely nicer.

**17. Who plays it?** `[meta]`
Just you? Friends? Family? Does it need to work on a phone, a laptop, both?

**18. Is there anything you want that Meridian doesn't have?** `[wildcard]`
The most useful question on here. Say the thing even if it sounds hard.
