# For AJ 👋

Hi AJ. This folder is yours.

Meridian Quest is a little town that runs in a phone browser. It works, you can walk
around it, and it was built to be *comfortable* — nothing chases you, nothing expires,
nothing you make gets taken away.

You want to make one too, open-world and cozy. Good news: **you don't have to build an
engine.** The same engine that runs Meridian will run your game — your town, your
people, your story. Meridian just becomes the example sitting next to you, and you can
copy anything out of it you like and change it however you want.

## What's in here

- **[QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md)** — things we need to know from you before building.
  Answer the ones you have opinions about; skip the rest. There are no wrong answers
  and nothing is locked in — everything here gets changed later when you play it and
  go "hmm, no."
- **[AFFECTED.md](AFFECTED.md)** — the boring-but-useful one: which parts of the code
  each of your answers actually changes. It exists so nobody has to guess how much
  work your answer is, and so you can see that "I want it to be a horse ranch" is
  genuinely a small ask and not a rude one.

## How this works

You answer in whatever form is easiest — voice notes, a text, scribbles, one word.
Someone turns that into a `content/<yourgame>/` folder: your maps, your people, your
quests, your words. The engine never changes. If you hate something later, we change
your folder and Meridian is untouched.

## Two things that are already true

**Your game can drop anything Meridian has.** The career stuff — the AI practice
quests, the report you can hand a hiring manager — is not part of the engine. If your
game is about a bakery and a mystery, none of that comes along.

**Your game can keep the good parts.** The cat you can pet, the trolley, the jacaranda
trees that sway, the character creator, the wardrobe, the day-and-night light, the
name easter eggs, the two-language support. All of it is available to borrow.
