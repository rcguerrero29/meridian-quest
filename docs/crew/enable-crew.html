Exactly what you do — the whole list

Not what the crew does. What lands on you, in order, with how long it takes and whether you can undo it. There are eleven things. Six of them you do once, ever. Two are clicks in a browser. Nothing on this list is code.

That is the whole step. It is a folder of short documents — one per settled decision, what was decided and why. No code, nothing to configure, nothing that can break the game.

Part two · optional, and unrelated — do it any time, or never. Nothing above depends on it.

7
you · 2 min
GitHub → Settings → Pages → Branch: main / root → Save.

One dropdown, one button, in the browser. About sixty seconds later the game is on a public URL you can open on your phone and add to your home screen. It plays offline after the first load.

What it changes for you: today you test by opening a file on your laptop. After this you test on the thing you actually play on, which is where every one of your reports has come from — the blur, the hair, the buttons, the tree.

2 minutesfully reversible — set it back to "None"it makes the game public
What Pages is not. It is not for the agents. They never touch it. It has nothing to do with worktrees, labels, or the crew — it is the same one setting it would have been a month ago, and the only reason it keeps coming up is that it is the difference between "it works on my laptop" and "it works." El Changarrito is never published by it — the town runs from your laptop only, and the smoke fails the build if anything of the town leaks into the public shell.

Part three · every time, forever — this is the part that never stops, and it is short

Your standing loop — four things
  1. Notice something and say it. Plain words. "It's still blurry in full screen." That is a complete bug report here.
  2. Answer a question, when there is one. Only ever taste, story, or how much — never anything a machine could have worked out. Usually zero or one per ticket.
  3. Look at what was built. A picture, a mock, a demo. Say "not that" freely; nothing is expensive to redo before merge.
  4. Merge. The only irreversible step in the whole system, and it is yours alone.
